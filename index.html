📖 How to Play Ugolki (Corners)

1. The Goal Move all your pieces across the 8x8 board into the opponent's starting corner.
2. Orthogonal Movement Pieces move orthogonally (Up, Down, Left, Right). Diagonal moves are strictly forbidden!
  • Step: Move 1 square into adjacent empty cell.
  • Jump: Jump over an adjacent piece into the empty square directly behind it.
3. Multi-Jumps (The Core Strategy!) You can chain multiple jumps together in a single turn to fly all the way across the board! You may stop at any intermediate jump landing.
4. Chess Clocks & Equal Turns Rule Just like chess, each player has a bank of time that counts down only on their turn. If a player runs out of time, they lose! Also, if Player 1 reaches the goal first, Player 2 receives one final turn to match for an honorable Draw.
5. Anti-Camping Vacate your home corner within the round limit (20-30 moves).
