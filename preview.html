📖 How to Play Ugolki (Corners)

1. The Goal Move all your pieces across the board into the opponent's starting corner.
2. Moving Pieces move orthogonally (Up, Down, Left, Right). No diagonal moves allowed!
  • Step: Move 1 square into adjacent empty cell.
  • Jump: Jump over an adjacent piece into the empty square directly behind it.
3. Multi-Jumps (The Core Strategy!) You can chain multiple jumps together in a single turn to fly all the way across the board! You may stop at any jump landing.
4. Equal Turns Rule Player 1 moves first. If Player 1 finishes, Player 2 gets one final turn to match. If both finish on that turn, it is a Draw!
5. Anti-Camping Vacate your starting corner within the round limit (20-30 moves).
